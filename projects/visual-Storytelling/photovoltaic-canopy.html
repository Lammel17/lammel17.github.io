Photovoltaic Canopy

Tags: video-production, davinci-resolve, interview

For the production of this video, I was part of a team of three interviewing Hartmut Gräter, Climate Protection Manager at Ravensburg-Weingarten University (RWU). The video presents a concept for transforming a small car park at RWU into a multifunctional building with a photovoltaic canopy. The challenge of the project was to plan the shooting day and the interview questions in a way that would provide good video material and create a natural feel. The video also features a drone shot combined with an animation showing what the building could look like.

I was involved in planning and editing the video using DaVinci Resolve, while also taking part in the interview.

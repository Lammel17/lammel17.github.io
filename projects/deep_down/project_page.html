Deep Down Aliens (Java)

Tags: java, coding, games, platformer, processing, pixel-art

Deep Down Aliens is a game prototype, an accumulation of all the knowledge from the prior Java exercise . You play as a blue alien in an environment resembling a cave. There are larger enemies on four legs, trying to hunt you down with their two sharp front legs.

Click on this Google Drive link to get to the playable prototype, extract the folder, and execute the .exe file.

The Challenge

The general idea was to create a simple platformer with responsive movement. Since Processing is not a game engine, I had to code the collision detection and physics behavior myself. It was quite an enjoyable challenge.

Features

The player can double jump, jump or fall through specific platforms, dash, and kick to fight against the enemies.

The Pixel Art and Level Design

For the pixel art and level design, I coded two separate programs where I could draw pixels in a grid and convert them into code, which could then be read by the game to build the sprites.

Memory Snake

Tags: card-game, tiles, clip-studio-paint

Memory Snake is a simple card game for two players, or rather, the concept for a card game. I developed the concept and rules and tested the game on volunteers. It proved to be an enjoyable game. I then designed the tiles as sections of a snake, hence the name.

The design for the tiles were drawn in Clip Studio Paint and printed out afterwards.

How is it played?

Both players draw the shape of the snake using which is possible to lay out with the given tiles (12), preferably on graph paper. One player then begins to guess the shape of their opponent's snake, which is hidden from them. The tail, the first tile, is placed correctly by the opponent, and the player then starts placing tiles to shape the snake. When a tile is placed incorrectly, the players switch roles, with the opponent now guessing the player's snake. Intersections are allowed, with intersecting tiles being placed on top of the non-intersecting tile.

The goal is to remember the previous steps of the correctly placed tiles after a switch and continue from there. When one player is able to correctly recreate the entire shape of their opponent's drawn down snake, they win.

Coast Guard Character

Tags: 3d, blender, character-modeling, retopology, texturing, substance-painter

Creating a character in a 3D environment like Blender comes with many challenges. I went from concept art to the final model, going through sculpting, retopology, texturing, and partly rigging. With the correct lighting, I was able to create very good shots of the character. It was a very enjoyable project.

The Concept

I started by drawing a concept for the character, whose fashion was inspired by Bloodborne's.

Retopology

I started by sculpting a body from the ground up, then I added a rough lump for the thick coat and sculpted it into the desired shape. After that, I started retopology by hand, intending not to get too much into detail, as that would be against its purpose. I am very happy with the result.

Texturing

For the textures, I used Adobe Substance Painter, which is an amazing tool. I would love to work more with it in the future, but sadly, Adobe's tools are pricey andmy projects are not heavily relied on it. in texturing.

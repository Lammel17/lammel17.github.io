Weapon Concept Collection

Tags: digital-art, sketches, concept-art, clip-studio-paint

Anyone who has played Bloodborne will know how fascinating its trick weapons are. I think their appeal comes from the plausible designs, where a single weapon can be used in two distinct forms or ways.

Between the thorughs of what game I might develop one day and what I could draw next, I found myself coming up with some interesting weapon designs. The sketches are not particularly polished, but they still capture the ideas and demonstrate some of the skills I have developed. I therefore wanted to include them in my portfolio.

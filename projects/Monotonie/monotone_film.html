Monotone - Short Film

Tags: film, davinci-resolve, audio-design, short-film, boredom

"Monotone" is a short film about an office worker trapped in a loop of intense boredom and monotony. When he faces the same boring routine of simply being at work and doing nothing, time appears to move slower and slower. Each minute becomes unbearable, until he snaps and throws the items on his desk. This outburst is the most interesting and exciting event of the day, calming him from the boredom, only to leave him back where he started.

About the production

This project was a group project consisting of four students, with a volunteer student as the actor. My role was to direct the filming and share responsibility for the video editing. I was also involved in developing ideas for portraying monotony, which was the most exciting part of the project for me.

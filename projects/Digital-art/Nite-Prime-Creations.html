Nite Prime Creations

Tags: digital-art, instagram, clip-studio-paint

Long before I even finished school, I started to follow my interest in digital art. With my first Wacom tablet, which I bought around 2014, I was immediately hooked on creating my own artworks. Since then, I have improved my skills and created several pieces of digital art.

Even though I never really took off with it, mostly because my interest was more in creating the art itself than sharing it on social media, I got a great deal of use out of it. It was my entryway to the University of Applied Sciences Ravensburg-Weingarten (RWU) in Germany, where I studied for 5 years and found my place at the intersection between design and technology.

About my Development

Since I started drawing with my Wacom tablet, I have used Clip Studio Paint as my drawing program. Over those many years of drawing, I have changed my approach several times. I started working with vector lines, but eventually ended up using rather sketchy brushes. I also tended to work with colors more in the past, as I found them to be the most frustrating part. It takes a lot more energy and time to finish a picture in color. Therefore, I have recently started enjoying it more when I keep my artwork colorless and monochrome. This way, I am able to create more finished works in a given amount of time, instead of fiddling with layer upon layer of color shades.

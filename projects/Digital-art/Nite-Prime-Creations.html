Tags: digital-art, instagram, clip-studio-paint

Long before I even finished school I started to follow my interest in digital art. With my first Wacom tablet, which I bought around 2014, I was immediately hooked on creating my own artworks. Since then have I impvoved and created several pieces of digital art.

Even if I never took of with it, mostly because my interest was more in creating the art itself than sharing it on social media, I got a great use out of it. It was my entryway to the University Ravensburg-Weigarten (RWU) in germany, where I studied for 5 years and found my place on a bridge between design and technic.

About my Development

Since I started drawing with my Wacom tablet I have used Clip Studio paint as drawing programm. In those many years of drawing I have changed my drawing approach often. I started working with vector lines, but ended with using rather sketchy brushes nowdays. Also I tended to work with colors more in the past, as I had the most frustration with them. It takes a lot more energy and time to finish a picture colored. Therefore I recently enjoy it more if i keep it colorless, monochrome. This way I am able to create more finished works in a given time, istead of fiddling with layers on layers of color shades.

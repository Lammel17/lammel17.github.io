My hearing disability

I was born in 2000 and attended a boarding school for children with hearing impairments. I have been hearing-impaired since birth and am accustomed to using hearing aids. As I grew older, however, my hearing gradually deteriorated, which eventually led me to undergo surgery to receive a cochlear implant (CI). Receiving the cochlear implant greatly improved both my ability and confidence to communicate with others. Today, I can communicate comfortably in everyday and professional environments, allowing me to work easily within different teams and working environments while bringing my own experiences and perspective with me.
